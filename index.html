Energy & Water

Are energy-efficiency systems (lighting, HVAC, DHW) in place to reduce consumption?

Yes. 100% of guestroom and public-space lighting fixtures are LED.

Source: Purchase Orders

Is an active water-conservation program in place (e.g. low-flow fixtures)?

Yes. Guestroom baths use low-flow fixtures following WaterSense/CALGreen certification standards. Products performed better than the standards:

  • Toilets use 20% less water
  • Faucets use 55% less water
  • Showerheads use 30% less water
Source: Specifications

Materials, Packaging & Sourcing

Does the packaging material contain recycled content? / sustainably sourced packaging?

Yes.

In the guestroom renovation:

  • 32% of spend and 39% of products by surface area used packaging with recycled content
  • 60% of spend and 72% of products by surface area used packaging with recycled or sustainably sourced raw materials

In the public space renovation:

  • 3% of spend and 9% of products by surface area used packaging with recycled content
  • 32% of spend and 55% of products by surface area used recycled or sustainably sourced materials
Source: Mindclick

Does the property take initiatives to reduce single-use products and packaging?

Yes. 65% of the products by surface area throughout the guestrooms/corridor were shipped in 100% recyclable packaging.

Source: Mindclick

Do you evaluate your supply chain based on ESG criteria?

Yes. FF&E purchasing representing 75% of spend and 93% of surface area in the guestrooms and public space was evaluated through the Mindclick Sustainability Assessment Program (MSAP):

MSAP covers:

  • materials, toxin avoidance, manufacturing practices, fair labor & human rights, packaging, circularity, contribution to hotel energy efficiency, waste reduction and water conservation, and guest health & wellness
Source: Mindclick

Does the property have an environmental procurement policy for its vendors?

Yes. Vendors are asked to have their products evaluated through the Mindclick Sustainability Assessment program (MSAP).

Source: Vendor Partner Letter

Do you have a documented record of responsible-sourcing initiatives?

Yes. We use Mindclick to guide our vendor selection and analyze the environmental and social impact of the products we purchase.

Source: Impact Brief

Environmental Programs

Please provide your top five environmental programs actively communicated.

Yes. We actively measure and report on how our purchasing choices impact hotel sustainability, including:

  • Guest Health: better indoor air quality and healthier, toxin-free products
  • Waste: including reduced and recyclable packaging and reuse of products
  • Energy efficiency: LED lighting and energy-efficient products
  • Water conservation: low-flow fixtures
  • Responsible sourcing: fair labor + human rights and environmentally responsible manufacturing and carbon emission reductions
Source: Mindclick

Guest Health

Does the property accommodate guests with chemical sensitivities and/or allergies?

Yes. Guestroom finishes are independently tested for low emissions and cleaner indoor air:

  • Carpet: wool/nylon, third-party tested to meet LEED VOC and formaldehyde limits.
  • Carpet padding: Green Label Plus certified, tested free of VOCs.
  • Wallcovering: Phillip Jeffries (tested free of VOCs), Koroseal (tested by Berkeley Analytical), and Wolf-Gordon (NSF-certified).
  • Paint: Sherwin-Williams, tested to meet LEED VOC limits.
  • Pillow fabric: OEKO-TEX and Greenguard certified, tested free of VOCs.
  • Mattress: CertiPUR certified, tested free of VOCs.

In the bath, ceramic and porcelain tile and vitreous-china fixtures are inherently free of VOCs, installed with no VOC adhesives or finishes.

Source: Mindclick (IAQ)
